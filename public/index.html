How access works

  1. There are two passcodes. The viewer passcode lets HR search and download. The editor passcode also allows adding, editing, importing and deleting.
  2. Passcodes are set in the Vercel project settings (VIEW_PASSCODE and EDIT_PASSCODE), never in this page. Change them there and redeploy.
  3. To sign everyone out at once, change SESSION_SECRET in Vercel and redeploy.
  4. Sessions end after a set number of hours, after 30 minutes without activity, or when someone selects Lock.
